Really Look for in Press Releases, based on interviews with journalists &#8212; including veteran reporters and editors &#8212; about how they evaluate press releases.</em></p><p><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/john-pacenti?utm_source=share&amp;utm_campaign=share_via&amp;utm_content=profile&amp;utm_medium=android_app">John Pacenti</a>, an Emmy&#8209;winning investigative journalist who has <a href="https://www.palmbeachpost.com/staff/5535867002/john-pacenti/">covered South Florida</a> for more than three decades, said the relationship between a PR representative and a reporter works best when both sides understand what the reporter needs: facts and access.</p><p>That expectation starts with the press release. &#8220;If I receive a press release that is missing critical information or doesn&#8217;t include contact details, I am suspicious of the release, the agency behind it, and the subject matter in general,&#8221; said Pacenti, who is president of First Way Communications.</p><p>A news release should serve as the reporter&#8217;s &#8220;elevator pitch&#8221; &#8212; self&#8209;contained, with all essential information and canned quotes &#8212; while still leaving room for follow&#8209;up interviews and additional reporting.</p><p>Pacenti pointed to two similar condominium developments in Delray Beach to show how this plays out. One project provided a thorough press release, with the media contact responding quickly. The contact also granted a tour and made business owners available for interviews.</p><p>The other offered poor contact information, conflicting details, and no returned calls, Pacenti said. &#8220;To this day, I still have not received any information.&#8221; </p><p>The difference shaped his perception of each project. The first development &#8220;went out of their way to provide information.&#8221; The second &#8220;acted like the reporter was a bother,&#8221; which raised red flags.</p><p>&#8220;As a reporter, it makes me highly suspicious that there is something wrong with the complex,&#8221; Pacenti said. &#8220;And it all started with the news release.&#8221;</p><p class="button-wrapper"><a class="button primary" href="https://substack.einpresswire.com/subscribe?"><span>Subscribe now</span></a></p><p></p>
