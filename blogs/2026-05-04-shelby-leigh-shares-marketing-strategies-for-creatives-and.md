When Shelby Leigh began sharing her work online nine years ago, she hoped to learn how to market her products and connect with others in her space. What she may not have expected is that she’d go on to inspire hundreds of creatives and sell more than 60,000 books.That experience led her to launch Marketing by Shelby, a platform that makes her marketing expertise accessible to artists, authors, entrepreneurs, and other creators.But one thing that sets Leigh apart from many creatives in the marketing space is her advocacy for mental health.
